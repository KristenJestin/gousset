What changes

  • The menu holds four things, as #105 shipped: the name and e-mail at its top (a heading, not a button), Settings, Keyboard shortcuts with its key, then Sign out under a rule. Sign out is a plain item: leaving is not destructive.
  • The menu is a floating surface (its own surface, edge and shadow), wider than the sidebar so the e-mail fits on one line; it opens upwards from the button, which stays pressed while it is open.
  • Settings has sections at the left: General, Your account, Categories, Rules, Data, then Users and MCP access under Administration, for the administrator only.
  • General holds what applies at once, inline, each a row with its label over one line of help and the control at the right: language (a select), colour scheme (System, Light, Dark), single-key shortcuts (a switch).
  • Your account holds the name and e-mail, the password (a button that opens the dialog, page 3's rules) and the connected MCP clients with Revoke; the consent screen (page 4) points here.
  • Phone: the More tab lists the screens the tab bar leaves out, then Settings and Keyboard shortcuts, then Sign out on its own; a settings section is a page of its own with a way back.

Rejected

  • Language and scheme kept in the menu as well: two places for one setting, and the menu grows back to what #105 removed.
  • Sign out in red: the menu would shout for the least used item.
  • Settings as tabs across the top: six sections, Users only for some, and the labels wrap on a phone.
  • Connected clients in General: they are the user's own, not the device's.

Motion

Open 0–150
The menu fades in and rises 4 px from the button, 150 ms ease-out.
Close 0–100
It fades out and sinks back 4 px, 100 ms ease-in; focus goes back to the button.
Scheme
Choosing a scheme changes the colours at once, no fade (a fade of every colour reads as a flicker).
Reduced
The menu is there, or not.

To decide

  • Today General also holds the connected clients and the switch of MCP access, and the password is a page of its own. Proposed: a Your account section for the password and the clients, and the switch under Administration, so General keeps only what applies to this device.
  • Whether Keyboard shortcuts stays in the More tab on a phone, where there is rarely a keyboard (#105 says it follows the same list: drawn so).
