  • Never wraps, never shrinks: a label that does not fit is shortened, or the row stacks (full width on a phone).

  • 36 or 32 px, both 14 px text (proposed: the small one was 12 px and read as a tag). An icon goes before the word.

What this page is

The rules every fix of the 0.2 pass applies, so that each screen is fixed the same way. The ratios are measured in the browser from the colours as they resolve in the chosen scheme: switch Light and Dark.

What changes

  • A third text level, --soft-foreground, between the text and the muted grey: a row's facts (account, period, format) no longer share the headers' grey, which is why every text looked the same.
  • Two state colours, --success and --warning, so that “imported” never borrows the income green, nor “to review” the spent orange.
  • The small button keeps 14 px text; 32 px high instead of 36, never smaller text.
  • The scrim --overlay behind sheets and dialogs (page 3).

Rejected

  • Telling levels apart by size only (13 px for facts): a size between Tailwind's steps, and 13 px Geist is blurry at 1×.
  • Bold (700) for emphasis: Geist at 700 is heavy beside 500 labels; 600 is the ceiling.
  • Lowering the muted grey's contrast to make room: it would fall under 4.5:1.

To decide

  • The four proposed tokens (--soft-foreground, --success, --warning, --overlay): each goes into globals.css with its pair in contrast.test.ts.
  • The small button's 14 px text.
