What changes

  • The filters are one 32 px row, no label above any field: each one says what it is by itself. The search has its icon and placeholder, the account and the period show their value (“All accounts”, “Any date”) with an icon, the amount range is one field pair under one word, Amount, min – max. Every field keeps an accessible name. No hint under a field: the date format is the calendar's placeholder.
  • “To review only” is a toggle button in the row, with its count; pressed, it turns violet. The count of lines and Clear filters sit on the line under the row, which says what is filtered in words.
  • Amounts by sign: money in is green (--money-income) with a +, money out stays in the text colour with a true minus (−). The sign is always written, so it reads without the colour, for screen readers and colour-blind eyes. Both pass 4.5:1 on the block in each scheme: the scale page measures them.
  • Three text levels in a row: the label in medium, the date and the account in the soft colour, the headers in muted 12 px.
  • The detail sits beside the list, never over it. The list makes room: its account column gives way while the detail is open (below 1440 px), never the amounts, which stay in their right-aligned column.
  • The detail reads as a sheet of facts: the label (14 px, it wraps), the amount large, then three groups (where and when · what the bank wrote · where it came from), names left in muted, values left-aligned after them, so long values wrap cleanly.
  • The open row is marked: a muted band and a 3 px violet edge, which stays until the detail has closed.
  • Phone: the search and a Filters button with the number of filters set; the filters open in a bottom sheet whose button says how many lines it will show. Rows are two lines (label, then day and account), grouped under the day. A line's detail is a bottom sheet.

Rejected

  • Labels above the filters (today): “Amount from” wraps, the date hints make some fields taller, the row breaks and the box floats alone below.
  • A Filters popover on desktop: one more click for the filters used most; the row fits from 1280 px. Between 1024 and 1279 px the period, the amount and the toggle go into a Filters button, as on the phone.
  • Money out in orange (the switch above shows it): nine rows out of ten turn orange and the list shouts; orange is “spent” in the month's charts, a role, not a sign.
  • The row growing into the panel (the shared layout of docs/design.md, “Opening a detail”): a layout animation scales the box, and its text with it; that is the squashed, ghosting text of #128. Its spirit stays: the row is marked, the panel opens beside it.
  • A panel sliding over the list from the right: it covers the amount column, which is the complaint.

Motion of the detail (#128)

Open 0–300
The detail's column widens from 0 to 360 px (+16 gap), ease-out: the list narrows with it. The width moves, never a scale, so no text is ever squashed.
Open 120–300
The content, laid out at its final width from the start, fades in and slides 8 px from the right (180 ms, ease-out), once there is room for it.
Close 0–120
The content fades out (120 ms, ease-in), first.
Close 100–320
Then the column closes (220 ms, ease-in); the row's mark leaves at the end.
Phone
The sheet slides up from the bottom edge (300 ms, ease-out) as the backdrop fades in (200 ms); it leaves downwards in 200 ms. It can be dragged down.
Reduced
Nothing moves: the detail is there, or not.

The tests (#128): the column's width at 0, 150 and 300 ms; the content's opacity 0 until 120 ms; no transform with a scale on the panel or its text at any frame; nothing under reduced motion.
