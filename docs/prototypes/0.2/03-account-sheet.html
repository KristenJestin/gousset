What changes

  • The sheet slides in from the right edge over a backdrop that dims the page; today it appears at once over an unchanged page. The backdrop is a dark scrim in both schemes (--overlay, proposed): the current light veil reads as “disabled” in light, and a white sheet loses its edge on it.
  • A press on the backdrop closes the sheet or the dialog, as ✕, Cancel and Escape do; while the form is sent it does nothing, and ✕ and Cancel are dimmed (#103). Try it with the switch above.
  • The submit button keeps its width while busy: the coin loader and “Adding…” take the label's place.
  • The dialog follows the same rules: the same backdrop, the same closing, the focus ring whole. On a phone both are a bottom sheet, its whole width (docs/design.md).
  • #122: the scrolling body carries the padding, not the sheet around it, so a focused field's 2 px ring and its 2 px offset lie inside the area that clips. The same rule for the dialog, the import's account question and every block that scrolls.
  • The form reads in two groups, the account then its start, with one hint where a choice needs it, and no label that wraps: pairs only where both labels are short.

Rejected

  • An inset focus ring (drawn inside the field): it never clips, but it sits on the text and the border, and differs from every other focus in the app.
  • Asking “Discard what you typed?” when the backdrop is pressed: a dialog over a sheet for a five-field form. Escape and the backdrop close, as everywhere else.
  • A backdrop that blurs the page: costly on a phone, and the page behind is still useful context.
  • The sheet pushing the page aside: the page reflows twice for a form that lasts seconds.

Motion (#121)

Open 0–300
The sheet slides from beyond the right edge to its place, 300 ms ease-out; the backdrop fades in over 200 ms. Focus goes to the first field.
Close 0–200
The sheet leaves to the right and the backdrop fades out, 200 ms ease-in; focus goes back to the button that opened it.
Dialog
Fades in and grows from 96 % in 200 ms, ease-out; leaves in 150 ms, ease-in. The scale moves the box once, never its text while it is read.
Phone
The bottom sheet rises 300 ms ease-out and leaves downwards in 200 ms.
Reduced
Nothing moves: the backdrop and the sheet are there, or not.

The tests (#121): the sheet's transform and the backdrop's opacity at 0, 150 and 300 ms, open and closed; none under reduced motion; a press on the backdrop closes, and does not while sending. (#122): the ring's box lies inside the scroller's visible box.

To decide

  • The scrim colour (--overlay, a new token): dark in both schemes, or the page's own colour at 70 % as today.
  • Pressing the backdrop with fields typed in: close and lose them (the ticket), or keep them until the page is left.
